# About the Author

Farhan Anjum is a Computer Science graduate and software engineer with practical experience working with software systems, testing and automation, databases, APIs, DevOps workflows, and Data Engineering projects.

His experience also includes FinTech systems and payment-related workflows, where reliability, correctness, integrations, and data handling are important parts of the engineering process.

This book brings together that practical engineering mindset with a structured approach to learning Data Engineering.

The focus is not on memorizing tools. It is on understanding systems, investigating existing code, building reliable pipelines, testing failure cases, and learning how to recover when things go wrong.
